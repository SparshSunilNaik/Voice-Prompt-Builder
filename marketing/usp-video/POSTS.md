## LinkedIn

> **Your AI is only as good as your prompt. So we stopped typing them.**
>
> Most people know *what* they want from ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini. Turning that into a precise prompt is the hard part. So we built **Voice Prompt Builder**, a Chrome extension that does it with you, out loud.
>
> **How it works**
> 🎙️ **Speak your idea.** No blank text box. Just explain the problem.
> 🧠 **It interviews you, hands-free.** It reads each multiple-choice question aloud and listens for your answer ("option two" works). Every question builds on your previous answer.
> ✨ **It picks the right prompting technique.** Zero-shot, one-shot, few-shot, chain-of-thought, decompose-and-verify. It chooses what fits your task instead of using one template for everything.
> 🔁 **It learns from you.** Hit Regenerate and it asks what was wrong. Say "too long" and it fixes it, then remembers for every future prompt. In our tests one round of feedback cut a prompt from 331 to 270 words.
> 🛡️ **You stay in control.** It drops the prompt into ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini and *never* hits send.
>
> It's in beta, and we'd love honest feedback from people who write prompts every day.
>
> 👉 Try it: https://sparshsunilnaik.github.io/Voice-Prompt-Builder/
>

## Posting tips for reach

- **Upload the MP4 natively** to both platforms; don't post a YouTube link. Native video gets far more reach.
- **LinkedIn:** set `poster.png` as the custom thumbnail. Put the link in the post (or the first comment if you prefer). Reply to early comments within the first hour.
- **X:** post the main tweet with the video, then the reply as a thread. Pin it to your profile.
- Most feeds autoplay muted, so the on-screen text still tells the story; the voice-over rewards people who tap for sound. On X, add a short "🔊 sound on" to the post if you like.
- Best times are usually weekday mornings in your audience's time zone (Tue–Thu).
